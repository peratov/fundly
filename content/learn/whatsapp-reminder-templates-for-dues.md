Most missed payments are forgotten, not refused. A well-timed, friendly message fixes the majority of them. Copy these templates, replace the bits in brackets, and adjust the tone to suit your group.

> **Rule of thumb:** reminders to everyone go in the group chat; anything about one person's payment goes **privately**.

## Monthly dues

**1. Friendly heads-up (3 days before)**
> Hi everyone 👋 Quick reminder that [Month] dues of [amount] are due by [day, time]. Pay by MoMo to [number/till] with reference *[member no] [MONTH]*. Thank you for keeping our fund strong! 💪

**2. Due today**
> Morning all ☀️ Today's the day for [Month] dues — [amount] by [time]. If you've already paid, thank you! 🙏

**3. Thank-you when most have paid**
> [Number] of [total] members have paid for [Month] — amazing! 🎉 If you haven't yet, there's still time until [time].

**4. Private nudge (1–3 days late)**
> Hi [Name], hope all is well. We haven't seen your [Month] contribution yet — no stress, just checking in. Let me know if anything's up. 🙂

**5. Private note on standing (after 2 missed months)**
> Hi [Name], a gentle note that [Month 1] and [Month 2] contributions are still outstanding, so your standing is now *behind* and new loans are paused until you catch up. Paying the missed months restores it straight away. Happy to talk if a plan would help.

## Merry-go-round

**6. Payout announcement**
> 🎉 Round [n] of [circle name] goes to *[Name]* on [date]! Everyone please send [amount] directly to [Name] ([number]) by [time] and drop your reference here.

**7. Who's still outstanding (group)**
> Round [n] update: thank you [names] 🙏 Still waiting on [names] — [amount] to [Name] by [time] please.

**8. Next up**
> Heads-up: next month's pot goes to *[Name]* on [date]. See the full order here: [link]

## Loans

**9. Instalment coming up (private)**
> Hi [Name], your instalment of [amount] on loan [ref] is due on [date]. You can pay by MoMo from your member portal. Thank you!

**10. Instalment missed (private)**
> Hi [Name], we didn't receive the [amount] instalment due on [date] for loan [ref]. Could you let me know when you expect to pay? If things are tight, let's talk about options.

**11. Loan approved (private)**
> Good news, [Name]! 🎉 Your [product] loan of [amount] has been approved and sent to your MoMo. Your first instalment of [amount] is due on [date].

## Meetings and welfare

**12. Meeting notice**
> 📅 Our [quarterly/annual] meeting is on [date] at [time], [place/link]. Agenda: [items]. Please confirm you'll attend 👍

**13. Welfare support notice**
> Dear all, with sadness we share that [member]'s [relation] has passed away. The welfare fund is supporting the family. Please keep them in your thoughts. 🙏

**14. Annual report shared**
> Our [year] report is ready: [link]. Highlights — [contributions], [loans repaid], [welfare paid]. Questions welcome before the AGM.

**15. Welcome a new member**
> Please welcome [Name] to [fund name]! 🎉 [Name], your member number is [no]. Dues are [amount] by the [day] of each month. Your invite link to the member portal: [link]
