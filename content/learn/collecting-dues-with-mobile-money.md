Cash at meetings is how money goes unrecorded. **Mobile money** — MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and AT Money in Ghana, M-Pesa in Kenya, and the growing range of wallets and instant bank transfers elsewhere — gives every payment a timestamp and reference, and lets members pay from wherever they are. Here's how to set it up properly.

## Three ways to collect

### 1. Pay the treasurer's personal number (avoid)
Easy to start, but it mixes group and personal money, depends on one person, and makes audits painful.

### 2. A dedicated group wallet or merchant account
A number or till used **only** for the fund. Much better: statements show only fund money, and several officials can have visibility. Check what your provider offers for groups and businesses.

### 3. Automated collection through your fund software
The member taps "Pay", approves a prompt on their phone with their own PIN, and the payment is **matched to their account automatically** — no screenshots, no manual ticking. This is how Fundly works: the payment lands, the contribution is recorded, shares are issued and the member's standing updates in one go.

## Make every payment identifiable

If payments aren't automated, agree a reference format:

> **Member number + month**, e.g. `M-0012 MAR`

and ask members to use it every time. Then once a month, the treasurer reconciles: every payment on the statement is matched to a member and month, and every recorded contribution has a matching payment.

## A monthly reconciliation routine

1. Download the wallet or account statement for the month.
2. Match each incoming payment to a member and month.
3. Investigate anything unmatched — wrong reference, wrong amount, unknown sender.
4. Check the closing balance equals the cash balance in your books.
5. Have the audit committee review it — see the [annual audit checklist](/learn/annual-audit-checklist-savings-group).

## Reminders that actually work

Most late payments are forgotten, not refused. A friendly nudge two or three days before the due date and another on the day make a big difference — copy our [WhatsApp reminder templates](/learn/whatsapp-reminder-templates-for-dues).

## Protect members from scams

Common mobile money scams target group members because they expect to send money regularly. Remind everyone:

- **Never share your PIN** — no official will ever ask for it.
- **Only approve prompts you started.** An unexpected "approve payment" request is a red flag.
- **Check the recipient name** before confirming a transfer.
- **Be wary of "wrong transfer, please send back" messages** — check your actual balance first.
- **Official numbers don't change by text message.** Confirm any new payment number in a meeting or with two officials.

## Refunds and reversals

Decide who handles overpayments, duplicates and payments to the wrong number, and record every refund in the books with a reason. It's a surprisingly common source of confusion.

## Common problems and quick fixes

| Problem | Quick fix |
|---|---|
| Payment with no reference | Ask the member for the transaction ID; match by amount and time |
| Paid to the wrong number | Contact the provider promptly; record once resolved |
| Double payment | Credit it to the next month or refund — record either way |
| Member can't find how to pay | Pin the payment details in the group chat |

Once money is coming in, [reconcile your mobile money statement](/learn/reconciling-mobile-money-statements) every month and issue [proper receipts](/learn/receipts-and-proof-of-payment), because screenshots alone aren't proof of payment.
