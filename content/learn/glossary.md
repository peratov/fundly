Savings groups have their own vocabulary, and different countries use different words for the same thing. Here's a plain-English reference. Terms link to fuller guides where we have them.

## Types of group

**ROSCA (rotating savings and credit association)** — A group where members pay in the same amount each round and one member takes the whole pot, rotating until everyone has had a turn. See [what is a merry-go-round](/learn/what-is-a-merry-go-round).

**Merry-go-round** — The everyday name for a ROSCA in many English-speaking countries.

**Susu** — Ghana's name for both rotating savings groups and collector-based savings. See the [susu guide](/learn/susu-ghana-guide).

**Chama** — Swahili for "group"; a savings, investment or welfare group in East Africa. See the [chama guide](/learn/chama-kenya-guide).

**Esusu / ajo** — Rotating and collector-based savings in Nigeria. See [esusu and ajo](/learn/esusu-ajo-nigeria-guide).

**Tontine, stokvel, tanda, committee** — Names for rotating savings circles in francophone Africa, South Africa, Mexico and South Asia respectively.

**ASCA (accumulating savings and credit association)** — A group that keeps savings in a shared fund and lends from it, instead of rotating a pot. See [ROSCA vs ASCA](/learn/merry-go-round-vs-savings-group).

**VSLA (village savings and loan association)** — A widely used ASCA model with meetings, a lockbox and share purchases.

**Welfare fund** — A group fund that supports members through bereavement, illness and emergencies.

## Contributions

**Contribution / dues** — The amount each member pays per period.

**Minimum contribution** — The least a member must pay each period; some funds allow more. See [how to set monthly dues](/learn/how-to-set-monthly-dues).

**Period / round** — One cycle of contributions: a week, fortnight or month.

**Pot** — In a merry-go-round, the total collected in a round and paid to one member.

**Payout order** — The sequence in which members receive the pot. See [deciding the payout order](/learn/merry-go-round-payout-order).

**Due day** — The day of the month after which a payment counts as late.

**Late** — Paid after the due day.

**Missed** — Not paid for that period at all.

**Standing** — A member's status based on their payment record — often *active*, *behind* or *suspended/voided*. See [when a member doesn't pay](/learn/when-a-member-doesnt-pay).

**Closing a period** — Marking everyone who didn't pay for a finished month as missed, so standings are accurate.

## Savings and shares

**Savings** — The part of a member's contributions the fund holds for them.

**Share** — A unit of ownership in the fund. Savings buy shares. See [shares and dividends](/learn/shares-and-dividends-explained).

**Share price** — What one share costs in a given period.

**Dividend** — Income (mainly loan interest) shared out to members in proportion to their shares.

**Bonus shares** — Shares granted as a reward (for example for years of service), recorded with a reason.

## Loans

**Loan product** — A type of loan with its own rate, maximum term and amount. See [running loans](/learn/how-to-run-loans-in-a-savings-group).

**Principal** — The amount borrowed.

**Flat interest** — Interest calculated on the original principal for the whole term. See [flat vs reducing balance](/learn/flat-vs-reducing-balance-interest).

**Reducing-balance interest** — Interest calculated on the amount still owed.

**Instalment** — One scheduled repayment.

**Repayment schedule** — The list of instalments and due dates.

**Arrears** — The amount a borrower is behind their schedule.

**Overdue** — A loan with arrears.

**Penalty** — A charge for late repayment. See [late loan repayments](/learn/handling-late-loan-repayments).

**Default** — When a borrower stops repaying and doesn't engage.

**Write-off** — Recording an unrecoverable loan as a loss in the books.

**Guarantor** — A person who agrees to cover a member's debt if they don't pay.

**Credit score** — A number summarising a member's reliability. See [fair credit scoring](/learn/fair-credit-scoring-for-members).

**Credit committee** — Members who review loan applications and recommend decisions.

## Governance and records

**Constitution** — The group's written rules. See the [constitution template](/learn/group-fund-constitution-template).

**AGM** — Annual general meeting of all members. See [running an AGM](/learn/how-to-run-an-agm).

**Signatory** — A person authorised to approve withdrawals from the group's account.

**Audit committee** — Members who independently check the books. See the [audit checklist](/learn/annual-audit-checklist-savings-group).

**Audit trail** — A record of who changed what, and when, that can't be edited.

**Reconciliation** — Matching the books against bank and mobile money statements.

**Double-entry bookkeeping** — Recording every transaction as equal debits and credits. See [double entry in plain English](/learn/double-entry-bookkeeping-for-savings-groups).

**Journal** — The list of all bookkeeping entries.

**Trial balance** — A list of all account balances; debits must equal credits.

**Welfare reserve** — Money set aside from premiums to pay welfare claims. See [welfare packages](/learn/welfare-packages-explained).

**Premium** — The part of a contribution that funds welfare cover.
